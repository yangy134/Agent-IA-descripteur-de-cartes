Can you describe the content of this map?

Map of a region of the United States of America with the city of Madison located between two lakes, Lake Mendota to the north and Lake Monona to the south. Further south, part of a third lake can be seen. Surrounding Lake Mendota are three medium-sized cities: Shorewood Hills, Middleton, and Maple Bluff. On the eastern shore of Lake Monona is the city of Monona. Major highways are numbered, with one running east to west between Lake Monona and the southernmost lake (Route 18). Three major north-south highways, one passing through the city of Madison between Lake Mendota and Lake Monona and the other two east of the lakes (151, 51, and 90). A large airport northeast of Lake Mendota. A hotel marked in pink in Monona. A botanical garden, hiking trails, a nature area to the south (Lewis Nine Springs) next to a farm park, a national park to the north, not far from a monastery. In the northeast corner, along Route 151, you can see the location of a large hospital, Madison Hospital.
