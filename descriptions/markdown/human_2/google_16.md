# Can you describe the content of this map?

Large-scale map section, certainly of the city of New Haven, because it shows Yale University (the old campus in the center of the map, the Sterling Memorial Library), the Yale bookstore a little further north, and certainly the new Yale campus in the northwest corner (drawing of a bed and just two words visible: New and Yale). There are two large green spaces in the center of the map, one to the north (a cemetery) and the other to the south (New Haven Green park). Next to the second one is a building, the Mead Visitor Center, which was probably designed to welcome visitors to the university. All the streets form a fairly regular grid pattern, oriented northwest/southeast. Two theaters are marked, along with a few places to eat or drink and a church.
