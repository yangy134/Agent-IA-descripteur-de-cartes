# Can you describe the content of this map?

Large-scale map of part of a large city, in this case Barcelona, Spain, as we can recognize the Plaça de Catalunya in the northwest of the map and the wide avenue known as La Rambla running southwards. The map is fairly uniform in terms of buildings. Three major streets stand out, colored yellow. Smaller streets are marked in bluish gray or white. Several symbols represent buildings: a cross for about fifteen churches, two masks to symbolize theaters, shopping carts for supermarkets, castle-like symbols for hotels, and columned temples for notable historical monuments.
