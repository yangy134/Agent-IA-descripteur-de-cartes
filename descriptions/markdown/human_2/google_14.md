# Can you describe the content of this map?
Fragment of Long Island with the town of Montauk and Fort Pond Lake in the center of the map. In the northwest corner and southeast corner, the Atlantic Ocean. In the northeast corner, part of a state park, Montauk Downs Park. The rest of the peninsula is built up. A main road crosses the peninsula from west to east, passing south of Montauk and Fort Pond Lake. A few hotels and restaurants are marked. The south of the peninsula has a large beach along the entire coastline.
