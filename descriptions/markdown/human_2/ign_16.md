# Can you describe the content of this map?
Large-scale map section of Procé Park in Nantes. The park is located in the center of the map, surrounded by Boulevard des Anglais, Boulevard Clovis Constant, Boulevard Albert Thomas, and Rue des Dervallières to the south. A river called the Chézine flows from south to northwest, widening after passing Procé Park. Inside the park, there are streets and paths represented by a single brown line and pedestrian paths represented by dotted lines. There is also a stadium. Surrounding the park is an urban area with streets (some of which are named), roundabouts (on the boulevards), and buildings. Two areas of buildings are colored purple to indicate two school complexes (to the east and northeast of the park).
