# Can you describe the content of this map?

Map of part of a large city in Spain, Barcelona, recognized by a famous monument named, in the center and slightly north of the map: the Sagrada Familia. In the northeast corner of the map, part of the port basins can be seen. Three major roads run from south to northeast, i.e., toward the center of Barcelona and the port area. Another major road, like a ring road, surrounds the city center. Two railway lines arrive, one from the south and the other from the east, at a station located in the heart of the city. Famous monuments and city districts are named. To the west and south of the map, beyond the main road/ring road, there are a few undeveloped green areas with some indications of relief on the peaks (144 m, 137 m). To the far west is a huge Repsol A Coruña refinery.
