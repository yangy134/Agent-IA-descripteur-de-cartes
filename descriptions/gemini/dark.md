# Can you describe the most visually salient elements of the map?

When analyzing the visual salience of a map, we\'re looking at what
elements immediately draw the viewer\'s attention. In this specific map,
several features stand out:

- **The Lake:**

  - The most prominent feature is undoubtedly \"The Lake\" itself. Its
    dark, contrasting shape against the surrounding even darker park
    area creates a strong visual focal point. The irregular shape of the
    lake also draws the eye.

- **The Dark Park Area:**

  - The overwhelming darkness of the park area surrounding the lake
    creates a strong \"figure-ground\" contrast. This contrast is a key
    element of visual salience. It allows the lake to stand out very
    distinctly.

- **The Street Grid:**

  - While also dark, the lines of the street grid, especially those
    labeled, provide a strong linear contrast to the organic shapes
    within the park. This contrast helps to define the parks boundaries.

- **Labeled Landmarks:**

  - The text labels, such as \"Cherry Hill Fountain\" and \"Bethesda
    Fountain,\" are visually salient because they break the uniformity
    of the dark background. Even though the text is small, the fact that
    it is text, makes it stand out to the human eye.

In essence, the map\'s strong contrast between the dark park area and
the slightly lighter lake, combined with the presence of text labels and
the straight lines of the street grid, creates a clear visual hierarchy.
