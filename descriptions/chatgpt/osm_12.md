- Orientation: North is at the top. The Cantabrian Sea borders the map

## 🏙️ Urban and Suburban Areas

- Santander is prominently placed near the center, along the coast,

- The Bay of Santander dominates the northeast quadrant, with inlets and

- Dotted blue lines indicate ferry routes or marine boundaries.
- Coastal towns like Somo and Pedreña are shown on the eastern shore.

## 🚗 Transportation

- Major Roads: The A-67, S-10, and N-611 are highly visible in red and
  pink hues. Their labels (e.g., 1, 3A, 10) make them prominent.
- Highway Junctions and interchanges are noticeable near El Astillero,
  Camargo, and Valdecilla.
- Railway lines (thin black lines) pass through key urban centers.
- Airport: A small airplane icon near Maliaño suggests the location of
  Santander Airport (SDR).

## 🌳 Green Areas and Parks

- The Macizo de Peña Cabarga natural area in the south (with a green
  mountain symbol) indicates higher terrain or protected parkland.
- Widespread light green areas denote forest, parkland, or rural zones.

## 🏞️ Topography and Water Bodies

- A few elevation labels like Monte and Cueto hint at hilly terrain.
- Rivers and small lakes are visible near El Astillero and Solares.
